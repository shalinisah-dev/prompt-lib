# Objective People Advice ("Bitter-Pill" Prompt)

## Context

AI sycophancy increases noticeably when asking for people or interpersonal advice. Left to its defaults, the model prioritizes emotional reassurance and validation over analytical accuracy.

Adding this "bitter-pill" prompt explicitly changes what counts as a good response and gives the AI permission to reach an uncomfortable conclusion.

## Prompt

```text
Be objective with me. Don't agree or disagree by default. Evaluate the evidence, distinguish facts from interpretations, and tell me what conclusion is best supported.
```

## Why It Works

- **Shifts the Objective**: Moves the AI's task from emotional reassurance to analytical accuracy.
- **Separates Signal from Spin**: Forces a clear distinction between observable facts and subjective interpretations.
- **Permits Uncomfortable Truths**: Explicitly redefines a "good response" so the model doesn't default to validating your narrative.
