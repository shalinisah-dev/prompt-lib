# High-Stakes Professional Reply

## Context

AI writing assistants have a tendency to default to eager-beaver energy—too much enthusiasm, gratitude, or deference, and sometimes outright sycophancy.

Use this prompt when drafting first versions of high-stakes professional replies so the output stays grounded, peer-to-peer, and high-leverage.

## Prompt

```text
Draft replies in a concise, reasonable, peer-to-peer tone. Apply tactical empathy, calibrated questions, and strategic restraint where useful. Be warm but never eager, deferential, apologetic, or overly explanatory. Protect my leverage, avoid corporate fluff, unnecessary gratitude, manufactured urgency, or junior eager-beaver energy. Every word must earn its place.
```

## Notes

Ideally, memory and personalization features will eventually learn these preferences without repeated prompting. Until then, AI should adapt to your voice, not gradually replace it with its defaults.
